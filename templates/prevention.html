01 Habitat & Behavioral Hygiene

WHO research indicates that 90% of the population breathes air containing high pollutant levels—pulmonary health starts at home.

Vapor Optimization

Maintain precision indoor humidity between 35-50%. Extremes in either direction disrupt the tracheobronchial clearance system.

Biological Barrier

The "Hand-to-Nares" route is the primary transmission path for 70% of respiratory pathogens. Standardize 20s hygiene routines.

HEPA (MERV 13) Filtration

Clean air particles down to 0.3 microns daily.

Deep Hydration Intake

Maintain 2.5L daily to keep pulmonary surfactant thin.

02 Pneumonia Strategy

Moving beyond treatment to high-fidelity preventive logic against bacterial and viral invasion.

Immunology Fact

PCV13 and PPSV23 vaccination series reduce invasive pneumococcal disease risk by over 75% in at-risk demographics.

Oral-Lung Axis

Periodontal pathogens are frequently micro-aspirated. Clinical oral hygiene reduces aspiration pneumonia risks by 34%.

Restorative Lung Exercises

Daily Protocol Incentive Spirometry

Practice deep, sustained inhalations (5-10 times/hour) to keep air sacs open and prevent fluid pooling.

Post-Scan Routine Pursed-Lip Stability

Inhale through the nose (2s) and exhale slowly through pursed lips (4s). This keeps airway pressure high.

03 TB Eradication Core

Tuberculosis requires extreme adherence to DOTS (Directly Observed Treatment Short-course).

The 6-Month Rule

TB medication must never be skipped for 180 consecutive days. Premature cessation leads to MDR-TB (Multi-Drug Resistant) pathogens.

Dietary Restoration

Lung cavity repair requires high protein density (1.5g per kg of body weight) and Vitamin D for macrophage activation.

Isolation Protocol Compliance

Maintain well-ventilated single occupancy during the first 3 weeks of treatment.

04 Asthma Control Matrix

Shifting from "Rescue" medicine to "Controller" logic according to GINA standards.

Pollution Triggers

PM2.5 levels above 35μg/m³ trigger immediate bronchial spasms. Use real-time AQI monitoring apps daily.

NSAID Warning

Up to 20% of asthmatics have sensitivity to Aspirin/NSAIDS, which can trigger life-threatening bronchospasms.

Peak Flow Tracking

Log morning/evening lung capacity. A 20% drop predicts an attack 48h early.
