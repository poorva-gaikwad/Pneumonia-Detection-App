Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this a replacement for a radiologist?
No. PneumoScan is a decision-support tool. All AI predictions must be verified by a board-certified professional.
What file types can I upload?
We support high-resolution JPG, PNG, and DICOM formats for scan analysis.
Is my patient data stored safely?
Yes, we use many security parameters and follow strict radiological privacy protocols.
How does the PWA / Offline mode work?
Once installed, essential app logic and your history are cached locally, allowing access without internet.
What is the 'Heatmap' feature?
It uses Grad-CAM technology to visually highlight the specific areas of the X-ray the AI used for its prediction.
How accurate is the detection?
Our current model achieves a 95.2% accuracy rate on validated clinical test sets.
Can I download reports for printing?
Yes, every analysis generates a detailed PDF report formatted for A4 printing.
Is there a cost to use the platform?
We offer a free tier for basic analysis and clinic-wide integration.
Does it work on smartphones?
Absolutely. The app is fully responsive and optimized for both iOS and Android browsers.
How do I register my clinic?
Simply click 'Get Started' and select the 'Clinical Account' option during registration.
