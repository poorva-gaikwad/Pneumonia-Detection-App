Patient Knowledge Hub

Is this a definitive medical diagnosis?
No. PneumoScan Pro is a Clinical Decision Support System. AI-generated inferences must always be validated by a board-certified physician or specialized radiologist.
How is my radiology data secured?
We utilize end-to-end TLS 1.3 encryption for data in transit and AES-256 bit encryption for storage. Session buffers are cleared automatically after inference processing is complete.
Can I download my results for external consultation?
Yes. Every successful diagnostic session generates a clinical-standard PDF report containing probability maps, confidence scores, and raw metadata for physician review.
