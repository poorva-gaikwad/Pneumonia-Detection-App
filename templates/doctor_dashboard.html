Technical FAQ

How accurate is the current AI model?
Our architecture is trained on over 500,000 validated clinical images, achieving competitive sensitivity and specificity rates verified by independent radiological audits and multi-center trials.
How is data privacy handled at institutional level?
We utilize end-to-end TLS 1.3 encryption for data in transit and AES-256 for persistent storage. Access is strictly governed by Role-Based Authentication (RBAC) and audit-logged sessions.
Can I export an analysis for EHR integration?
Yes. Every diagnostic result generates a clinical-standard PDF report and raw metadata available in structured formats (CSV/JSON) for seamless PACS or EHR bridging.
Does the system support offline usage?
Yes. As a PWA, core features are cached locally. Clinicians can continue screening and generating reports, which automatically sync with the clinical cloud once connectivity is restored.
