01

Pneumonia

Alveolar Inflammation & Fluid Infiltration

AI Confidence: 98% Code J18
Clinical Symptoms
  • High fever, shaking chills, and systemic fatigue.
  • Cough producing yellow, green, or bloody mucus.
  • Sharp chest pain during deep inhalation (Pleurisy).
  • Rapid, shallow breathing and tachycardia.
Priority Risk Factors
  • Age Demographics: Infants <2y and Adults >65y.
  • Immunocompromise: Recent viral infection (Flu/COVID).
  • Environment: Chronic smoking (Cilia paralysis).
  • Clinical: Difficulty swallowing (Aspiration risks).

AI Detection Technique: Adaptive Consolidative Mapping

The system utilizes a U-Net inspired architecture to segment lung fields. It specifically scans for Consolidation (solidification of lung tissue) and Air Bronchograms. Using pixel-intensity variance, the AI differentiates between viral "patchy" opacities and bacterial "lobar" solidifications.

02

Tuberculosis

Mycobacterial Pathogenesis & Cavitation

AI Sensitivity: High Code A15
Clinical Symptoms
  • Persistent cough lasting more than 3 consecutive weeks.
  • Hemoptysis (Sputum streaked with blood).
  • Drenching night sweats and rapid weight loss.
  • Chronic chest pain and persistent afternoon fevers.
Priority Risk Factors
  • Recent travel to WHO-classified high-burden TB zones.
  • HIV/COPD or conditions suppressing cellular immunity.
  • Close contact with active pulmonary TB cases.
  • Substance misuse and severe malnutrition.

AI Detection Technique: Apical Infiltrate Recognition

Detection focused on Apical segments of the upper lobes. The AI identifies Ghon Complexes (calcified scars) and Cavitations. Using edge-detection kernels, it isolates irregular thick-walled cavities and miliary (seed-like) patterns that standard radiology might overlook in early stages.

04

Asthma

Airway Hyper-Responsiveness

Risk Predictive Code J45
Clinical Symptoms
  • Whistling/Wheezing sound when exhaling.
  • Attack triggers following exercise or cold air exposure.
  • Difficulty sleeping due to nocturnal coughing.
  • Chest tightness feeling like an "invisible weight."
Priority Risk Factors
  • Atopy: Presence of Hay Fever or Eczema.
  • Obesity: Chronic low-grade systemic inflammation.
  • Pollution: High PM2.5/AQI exposure in urban environments.
  • Maternal smoking during pregnancy or childhood exposure.

AI Detection Technique: Hyperinflation & Risk Vector Scoring

As Asthma leaves limited radiological evidence, the AI scans for Lung Hyperinflation (flattened diaphragm) on radiographs. Primarily, it uses Bayesian Risk Scoring—integrating patient vitals, environmental AQI metadata, and symptom frequency to predict asthma exacerbation likelihood.
